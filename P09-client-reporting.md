# P09 — Client Performance Reporting

## Workflow
Client Ops & Reporting

## Problem Solved
Turning metrics into a client-ready narrative is slow and inconsistent.

## Prompt
> Given this data: [metrics], write a 150-word client summary: what happened, why, one next step. No jargon.

## Automation Potential
High — standardises tone and frees analyst time.

## Risks & Limitations
- Can misread trends or causation.
- Figures need human verification.

## Version History
### v1.0
Initial tested version.

### v1.1
Added a word limit, what/why/next-step structure, and no-jargon rule.

## Iteration Rationale
The changes make reporting shorter, clearer and more consistent for clients.
