# P10 — Reputation / Crisis Response

## Workflow
Reputation & Risk

## Problem Solved
Slow or inconsistent responses can escalate reputational damage.

## Prompt
> Draft 2 response options (professional, empathetic, ≤80 words) to this review [paste], for [client]. No liability admissions. Check both against [client]'s tone guide and revise any that don't fit.

## Automation Potential
Low-medium — drafts only; always needs senior approval.

## Risks & Limitations
- High reputational and legal risk if unsupervised.
- Never auto-publish.

## Version History
### v1.0
Initial tested version.

### v1.2
Added format constraints and a refinement/self-check step against the tone guide.

## Iteration Rationale
The self-check helps the draft fit the client's tone guide before human approval.
