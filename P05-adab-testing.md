# P05 — Paid Ad A/B Testing

## Workflow
Content Production

## Problem Solved
Manual variant-writing limits test volume and slows launches.

## Prompt
Serve as [client's] paid media copywriter. Make five different ad variations to promote [offer] to [audience] on [platform]. A headline of no more than thirty characters and a description of no more than ninety characters are required for every variation. Keeping the primary offer constant, test the designated angle [angle] across various hooks, perks, objections, or CTAs. High — more variants can be tested without extra headcount.
