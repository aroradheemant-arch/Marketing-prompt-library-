# P04 — Social Captions

## Workflow
Content Production

## Problem Solved
Manual captions take approximately 20–30 minutes per client per day.

## Prompt
> Social copywriter for [client]. Write 3 IG captions (≤150 chars) for [topic], tone [X], + 2 hashtags + CTA.

## Automation Potential
High — approximately 70% faster first drafts.

## Risks & Limitations
- May miss brand nuance.
- Human review is required before posting.

## Iteration Rationale
The changes were intended to make outputs more consistent and reduce manual editing.
