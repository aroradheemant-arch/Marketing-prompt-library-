# P02 — Audience Persona Synthesis

## Workflow
Research & Strategy

## Problem Solved
Turning raw research into usable personas is slow.

## Prompt
You are a senior marketing strategist and audience researcher. Using only the research notes supplied below, synthesise one evidence-grounded audience persona.
Research notes:
[PASTE RESEARCH NOTES HERE]
Do not invent, assume, or infer demographic, behavioural, psychographic, socioeconomic, geographic, or lifestyle details that are not supported by the supplied research. If important information is missing, explicitly state “Not established by the research.”
1. Persona Synthesis
Create one concise persona based strictly on recurring patterns, needs, behaviours, motivations, barriers, and preferences found in the research.
Include:
Persona name/label — use a descriptive label rather than inventing a fictional identity.
Primary goals
Key needs
Pain points/frustrations
Motivations
Barriers or objections
Relevant behaviours
Preferred channels/touchpoints
Information or content needs
Purchase/decision considerations, if supported
Clearly distinguish between:
Evidence: directly supported by the research.
Interpretation: a reasonable strategic interpretation of the evidence.
Unknown: information that cannot be established from the research.
2. RACE Framework Application
Apply the RACE Framework — Reach, Act, Convert, Engage to the persona.
Reach:
Identify where and how this audience is most likely to encounter the brand or relevant information based only on the research.
Relevant channels
Search/social/content touchpoints
Awareness needs
Appropriate reach message or content angle
Act:
Identify what would encourage the audience to interact, investigate, or take a micro-conversion.
Key questions they need answered
Content they are likely to engage with
Calls to action
Friction points
Convert:
Identify what could move the persona toward a purchase, enquiry, registration, booking, or other primary conversion.
Decision drivers
Objections/barriers
Trust requirements
Conversion messaging
Useful conversion assets
Engage:
Identify what could encourage repeat engagement, retention, advocacy, or loyalty.
Post-purchase communication needs
Relevant content
Retention opportunities
Community/relationship opportunities
Advocacy opportunities
Do not force a RACE stage where the research provides insufficient evidence. Mark unsupported areas as “Not established by the research.”
## Automation Potential
Medium — speeds synthesis; strategist validates against reality.

## Risks & Limitations
- Risk of over-generalising from a small sample.
- Persona should be validated against the supplied research.

## Version History
### v1.0
Initial tested version.

### v1.2
Added a grounding constraint after v1.0 invented demographic detail not present in the source notes.

## Iteration Rationale
The grounding constraint reduces unsupported assumptions and keeps the persona tied to the supplied research.
