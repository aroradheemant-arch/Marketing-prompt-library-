# P02 — Audience Persona Synthesis

## Workflow
Research & Strategy

## Problem Solved
Turning raw research into usable personas is slow.

## Prompt
You are a senior marketing strategist and audience researcher. Using only the research notes supplied below, synthesise one evidence-grounded audience persona.
Research notes:
[PASTE RESEARCH NOTES HERE]
Do not invent, assume, or infer demographic, behavioural, psychographic, socioeconomic, geographic, or lifestyle details that are not supported by the supplied research. If important information is missing, explicitly state “Not established by the research.”
 Persona Synthesis
Create one concise persona based strictly on recurring patterns, needs, behaviours, motivations, barriers, and preferences found in the research.
Include:
Persona name/label — use a descriptive label rather than inventing a fictional identity.
Primary goals
Key needs
Pain points/frustrations
Motivations
Barriers or objections
Relevant behaviours
Preferred channels/touchpoints
Information or content needs
Purchase/decision considerations, if supported
Clearly distinguish between:
Evidence: directly supported by the research.
Interpretation: a reasonable strategic interpretation of the evidence.
Unknown: information that cannot be established from the research.


## Risks & Limitations
- Risk of over-generalising from a small sample.
- Persona should be validated against the supplied research.

## Version History
### v1.0
Initial tested version.

### v1.2
Added a grounding constraint after v1.0 invented demographic detail not present in the source notes.

## Iteration Rationale
The grounding constraint reduces unsupported assumptions and keeps the persona tied to the supplied research.
