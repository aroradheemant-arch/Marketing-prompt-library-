# P01 — Competitor Research

## Workflow
Research & Strategy

## Problem Solved
Manual competitor tracking is time-heavy and inconsistent.

## Prompt

Examine the posts, pages, or research notes provided by the competitor. Note any themes in the messages used, the methods of audience engagement, posting frequency, format of the content, and any campaign strategies used. Examine the differences between competitors and [client] and find one gap or opportunity that the [client] can capitalize on strategically. Use only the provided evidence to support conclusions and do not make unfounded assumptions or note missing data.
## Automation Potential
Medium — solid first pass; strategist adds judgement.

## Risks & Limitations
- Cannot verify live data automatically.
- May cite stale campaigns.
- Strategist should validate findings before using them.
