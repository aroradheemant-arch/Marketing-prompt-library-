# P08 — Meeting Follow-Up

## Workflow
Client Ops & Reporting

## Problem Solved
Follow-ups are often delayed or inconsistent after calls.

## Prompt
> From these notes: [paste], list action items (owner, due date), then draft a 100-word client follow-up email.

## Automation Potential
High — near-instant, consistent follow-up.

## Risks & Limitations
- May miss meeting nuance or tone.
- Needs a human read-through.

## Version History
### v1.0
Initial tested version.

### v1.1
Decomposed the workflow into action-item extraction followed by email drafting.

## Iteration Rationale
The decomposition helps ensure action items are identified before the follow-up email is drafted.
