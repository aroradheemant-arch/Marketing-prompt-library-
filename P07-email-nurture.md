# P07 — Email Nurture Sequence

## Workflow
Content Production

## Problem Solved
Email sequences are repetitive to write for each client and campaign.

## Prompt
Act as an email marketing strategist for [client]. Draft a 3-email nurture sequence that moves [audience] from [trigger] to [goal]. Write a clear subject line and approximately 100 words per email. Structure the sequence progressively: Email 1 builds awareness, Email 2 addresses key needs or objections, and Email 3 drives the desired action. Maintain [client]’s tone of voice, include one clear CTA per email, and avoid unsupported claims, repetition, and unnecessary urgency. Ensure each email logically builds on the previous one and supports the overall conversion goal.

## Automation Potential
High — fast drafts; needs a brand-voice pass.

## Risks & Limitations
- Generic tone risk.
- Must meet consent and anti-spam rules.
